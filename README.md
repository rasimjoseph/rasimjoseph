# Hi, my name is Rasim. 
I'm a PhD student in the Department of Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering at Tulane University. My research centers on the systems of genes and gene regulators (aka gene networks) governing how cells behave and how they shape cell-to-cell interactions in cancer. I combine wet-lab experiments with computational modeling to study how these networks shape the tumor microenvironment and tumor progression.  <br>

My current focus is on identifying the mechanisms underlying phenotypic shifts in immune cell populations of the glioblastoma tumor microenvironment. <br>
